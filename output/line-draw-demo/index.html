Slide 1 of 5

One line. Five slides.

The line behind this card is a single continuous stroke. It does not reset per slide — as you move forward it keeps drawing itself from exactly where it left off. Press Next to continue the line.

Slide 2 of 5

It continues, it doesn't restart.

Watch the pen resume from the last point and draw the next segment. Go Back and the line retracts — the drawing is one shared object, not five separate clips.

Slide 3 of 5

Ambient, not the subject.

It sits at low opacity behind your content and ignores clicks, so it reads as texture — part of the background elements — never competing with the lesson.

Slide 4 of 5

Themeable by code.

The stroke uses your theme colors (here a gradient). Swap the path for a bird, a wave, or your own one-line illustration — same engine.

Slide 5 of 5

The drawing is complete.

By the final slide the whole illustration is revealed — the sun loop closes the composition. Across separate SCO pages, a shared persistKey keeps it going as one unbroken line.
