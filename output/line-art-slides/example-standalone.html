One-Line Wildlife Next: the line becomes the next animal
Chapter 1 of 5

The cat

The line draws itself into the first animal. Every slide, this same unbroken line reshapes into a new creature — one continuous stroke, no cuts, the drawing itself is the visual.

Chapter 2 of 5

The swan

Press Next and watch the cat flow into a swan on the water. The tail glides off both edges of the frame, so the line feels like it runs through the whole scene.

Chapter 3 of 5

The fish

The same stroke dives underwater and becomes a fish. Going back re-morphs to the previous animal, so the line always matches the current slide's content.

Chapter 4 of 5

The bird

Up out of the water, the line spreads two wings and takes flight — the transition itself carries the meaning from one subject to the next.

Chapter 5 of 5

The whale

Finally the line surfaces as a whale. Swap any animal by dropping a new single-line path into the shapes array — the engine draws and morphs it automatically.
